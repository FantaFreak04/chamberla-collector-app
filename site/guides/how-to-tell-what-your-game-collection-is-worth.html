How to Work Out What Your Game Collection Is Worth

Collection value is an estimate, not a fixed number. Prices move, and what a game sells for depends heavily on its condition.

Price by condition

Loose, complete in box and sealed copies of the same game can differ by a large amount. Always price the condition you actually own.

Use sold prices

Asking prices are wishes. Recent sold prices tell you what buyers paid. Price tracking sites such as PriceCharting aggregate these.

Refresh now and then

Retro prices can swing quickly. Chamberla Collector has a refresh option for prices, and shows your total by console.

Treat the total as a guide

If you plan to insure or sell, check individual high-value games by hand.
