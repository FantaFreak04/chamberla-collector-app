How to Find Retro Game Markets and Expos Near You

Retro game markets, swap meets and conventions are the best places to find physical games in person. They are also easy to miss, because many are run by small organizers and announced only a few months ahead.

Know the types

Big conventions have vendor halls, but the best deals for collectors are often at smaller retro game expos, gaming markets and swap meets, where most tables sell games. Anime and comic cons can have game sellers, but it varies.

Where to look

Organizer pages and ticket sites list most events, and collector groups share dates early. Chamberla Collector gathers game events from around the world in one list, with a filter by region, so you can see what is on near you.

Before you go

Check the date on the organizer's page, since dates can change. Decide what you are hunting for, bring a list of the games you already own, and note the condition and price you will accept. A checklist on your phone avoids buying a game twice.

Bring a friend

Going with someone makes it easier to split travel and compare finds. In the app you can pin an event, show it on your profile, and invite a friend.
