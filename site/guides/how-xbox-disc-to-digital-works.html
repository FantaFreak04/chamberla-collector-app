How Xbox Disc to Digital Works

Disc to digital lets you insert a supported Xbox One or Xbox Series disc into your console and get a digital copy of the game in your library. The idea is simple, but which games qualify is not always obvious.

How it works

Sign in to the console with your Microsoft account and insert the disc while connected to the internet. If the game is supported, the console offers a digital copy. Insert the disc to see what yours offers, because results can differ by edition and can change over time.

Not every game qualifies

Some games, such as certain editions, bundles or titles with special licensing, do not get a digital copy. Others depend on the exact edition printed on the disc.

Check before you buy or sell

If you are buying used games to go digital, or deciding whether a disc is worth keeping, it helps to know the result first.
