Chamberla Collector Rules and Terms

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Chamberla Collector is a hobby app for tracking and verifying your actual physical video game collection and sharing it with your friends. It's run by one individual developer. By creating an account you agree to these rules and they are written in plain English on purpose.

Who can use it

You must be 13 or over. You are responsible for your account and for anything that you do under it. Keep your sign in details private.

Be decent

Do not use the app to:

  • Harass, threaten or bully anyone, or share any hate content directed at an individual or group.
  • Post anything that is sexually explicit or that in any way sexualises or puts minors at risk.
  • Share someone's private details (e.g. their address or phone number) without their permission.
  • Pretend to be someone else.
  • Send spam, scams or unwanted bulk messages.
  • Break the law or incite others to break it.

Be honest about your collection

  • Don't fob off with verification in any form. No made up barcodes or labels and no scanning games that you don't have. Verification simply proves you've scanned something, it doesn't prove the game is genuine or in good condition.
  • Don't try and work around the limits, don't go fiddling with other people's data and don't go probing the service to see if you can find any weaknesses. If you do find a security issue please email us.

Listings and loans between users

The app enables users to list games and lend games to each other. Chamberla Collector doesn't handle any payments, shipping or returns and makes no guarantees as to any listing, buyer, seller or borrower. The deals are between you and the other person. Don't list any stolen, counterfeit or illegal goods. Be very careful, meet safely and use a payment method that protects you.

Reporting and blocking

You can block anyone and report any user from either their profile or a message. Reports are reviewed by the developer and a reported message will be available to the reviewer even if the sender subsequently deletes it.

What can happen if rules are broken

An account may receive a warning, a temporary suspension or a permanent ban depending on what occurs. A suspended account can still read but is not permitted to post, message, comment, list or edit its profile. Content which is in breach of the rules can be removed. A suspended or banned account cannot be deleted by the owner whilst the suspension is in force and so reports relating to it will be kept.

Appeals

If you think a suspension or ban was a mistake then please email support@chamberlacollector.com from the address on your account with a brief explanation. All appeals are read but there is no set schedule on which a decision will be made.

Your content

What you post remains yours. By posting it you're allowing the app to store it and show it to the people you've shared it with so that the app can function. You can delete your content or indeed your entire account from the app at any time.

No guarantees

The app is provided as is, free of charge and may change, have bugs and be unavailable at any time. Game prices, lists and disc to digital info is sourced from public and community sources and may be incorrect. You should not rely on it in any way to make a purchase decision without checking it yourself. To the extent permitted by law the developer is not liable for any losses incurred as a result of the use of the app.

Changes

If the rules change then the date above will change and significant changes will be in the app's patch notes. By continuing to use the app after a change you are accepting that change.
