Chamberla Collector Rules and Terms

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Chamberla Collector is a hobby app for tracking and verifying a physical video game collection and sharing it with friends. It is run by an individual developer. By creating an account you agree to these rules. They are written in plain language on purpose.

Who can use it

You must be 13 or older. You are responsible for your account and for what happens under it. Keep your sign-in private.

Be decent

Do not use the app to:

  • Harass, threaten, or bully anyone, or share hateful content aimed at a person or group.
  • Post sexual content, or anything that sexualizes or endangers minors.
  • Share someone's private information, such as their address or phone number, without their consent.
  • Pretend to be another person or organization.
  • Send spam, scams, or unwanted bulk messages.
  • Break the law or encourage others to.

Be honest about your collection

  • Do not fake verification: no made-up barcodes or labels, and no scanning games you do not own. Verification shows you scanned something, not that the game is genuine or in good condition.
  • Do not try to get around limits, tamper with other users' data, or probe the service for weaknesses. If you find a security problem, please email us instead.

Listings and loans between users

The app lets users list games and lend games to each other. Chamberla Collector does not handle payments, shipping, or returns, and does not guarantee any listing, buyer, seller, or borrower. Deals are between you and the other person. Do not list stolen, counterfeit, or illegal items. Be careful, meet safely, and use payment methods that protect you.

Reporting and blocking

You can block anyone and report any user from their profile or a message. Reports are reviewed by the developer. A reported message stays available to the reviewer even if the sender deletes it.

What can happen if rules are broken

Depending on what happened, an account may get a warning, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban. A suspended account can still read but cannot post, message, comment, list, or edit its profile. Content that breaks these rules may be removed. A suspended or banned account cannot be deleted by its owner while the suspension is active, so that reports about it are kept.

Appeals

If you think a suspension or ban was a mistake, email support@chamberlacollector.com from the address on your account with a short explanation. Each appeal is read, but a decision is not guaranteed on a set schedule.

Your content

What you post stays yours. By posting it you let the app store it and show it to the people you shared it with, so the app can work. You can delete your content, or your whole account, from the app.

No guarantees

The app is provided as is, free, and may change, have bugs, or be unavailable. Game prices, lists, and disc-to-digital information come from public or community sources and may be wrong. Do not rely on them for a purchase decision without checking. To the extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for losses from using the app.

Changes

If these rules change, the date above will change and notable changes will appear in the app's patch notes. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.
